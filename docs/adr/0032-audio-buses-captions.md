# ADR 0032: Audio has buses, positional sound and captions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Deciders: the R-Type team
- Discussion id: Block 9

## Context

Audio settings and visual cues for sounds are accessibility requirements; one plugin serves both backends (ADR 0008).

## Decision

`IAudio` creates sounds, streams music, plays voices with `PlayParams` (volume, pitch, pan, bus, optional world position, loop, caption), controls four buses (Master, Music, Effects, Interface) and the listener. A non-empty caption publishes a `Caption` event, generated by the engine so every backend supports it.

## Alternatives considered

- **One global volume:** players cannot lower music without losing effects.

## Consequences

- Bus volumes are console variables saved in user settings.
- Games show captions in their HUD.
